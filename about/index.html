About

Vignesh Swaminathan, PE

Licensed civil engineer. Known online as Mr. Barricade. I explain how streets get designed, and how you can change yours.

I'm Vignesh Swaminathan, a licensed civil engineer (PE). Online I go by Mr. Barricade, and I explain how streets get designed: why a crosswalk looks the way it does, who decides the speed limit, where the rainwater goes, and how you can ask your city for something better.

I have spent more than a decade in active transportation and highway design. I have designed more than 100 protected corners in California, and I contributed to NACTO's design guide, Don't Give Up at the Intersection.

Where the name comes from. Early in my career I worked on city traffic control, closing streets with barricades for marathons, parades and festivals. People started calling me Mr. Barricade, and it stuck.

Why I make videos. In 2020 I started posting short videos to show people the inside of street design. About 1.6 million people now follow along on TikTok, and the same lessons go out on Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and X.

Both sides of the dais. I have served as chair of the Cupertino Sustainability Commission and of a Bay Area transit agency's citizens advisory committee. That is why so much of what I teach is about public meetings, public comment, and getting a request in front of the right person.

Background. I studied civil engineering at San Jose State University, with a B.S. and an M.S. focused on transportation. In 2025 I spoke twice at the Strong Towns National Gathering in Providence.
