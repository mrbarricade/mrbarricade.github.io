What is a quick build bike lane?

By Vignesh Swaminathan, PE (Mr. Barricade)

A quick build bike lane is built mainly with low cost materials such as paint, flexible posts, low curbs, and rubber speed humps, so a city can change a street fast. Arlington County, Virginia says its quick build projects using flex posts, bollards, and pavement markings are typically built within several months. Some quick build projects also use concrete or other permanent materials.

The materials

NACTO's Don't Give Up at the Intersection describes a range of tools, from quick build to permanent:

  • Markings and color, often green, to show the bike path through conflict areas.
  • Paint and posts. Bollards and other low vertical elements, which NACTO says can be installed relatively quickly and at low cost.
  • Low delineators, such as temporary curbs.
  • Mountable rubber speed humps, an off the shelf alternative to raised truck aprons, secured to asphalt or concrete.
  • Surface concrete and modular islands, which NACTO says can often be built by sidewalk or highway repair crews.
  • Full construction, such as raised bike lanes and full truck aprons, for long lasting projects.

How long it takes

Arlington County splits its quick build work in two. Projects with flex posts, bollards, and markings are low cost and typically built within several months. Quick build construction projects with concrete, asphalt, or beacons are typically under $250,000, are planned to go from idea to finished within 1 to 3 years, and are typically built within two years.

Do posts actually help?

Yes, according to FHWA's 2023 study of separated bike lanes. Treatments with flexible delineator posts were among the most effective it measured: about 53 percent fewer bike crashes compared with traditional or painted buffer lanes.

Paint first, concrete later

NACTO shows protected intersections built with interim materials, using flexible posts and modular speed bumps where a concrete corner would normally go. My engineering view: quick build is a way to test a layout on a real street and adjust it before pouring concrete.

Frequently asked questions

What materials are used in quick build bike lanes?

Paint and green markings, flexible posts and bollards, low curbs, mountable rubber speed humps, and sometimes surface concrete or modular islands.

How long does a quick build project take?

Arlington County says its projects with posts, bollards, and markings are typically built within several months, and its quick build construction projects within about two years.

Are quick build bike lanes permanent?

They can be either. Arlington's quick build projects may use tactical materials like posts and paint or permanent materials like concrete, and NACTO describes a range from quick build to full construction.
