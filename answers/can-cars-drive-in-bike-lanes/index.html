Can cars drive or park in a bike lane?

By Vignesh Swaminathan, PE (Mr. Barricade)

Usually not. In California, Vehicle Code 21209 lets a driver use a bike lane only to park where parking is permitted, to enter or leave the roadway, or to prepare for a turn within 200 feet of the intersection. Other states write their own rules, so check your state's vehicle code.

The rule for drivers in California

Vehicle Code 21209 says no one may drive a motor vehicle in a bike lane except:

  • To park where parking is permitted.
  • To enter or leave the roadway, such as at a driveway.
  • To prepare for a turn within 200 feet of the intersection.

Passing slow traffic is not on that list. The same section allows a motorized bicycle, as California law defines it, in a bike lane at a reasonable speed that does not endanger people biking.

Where drivers must cross a bike lane to reach a turn lane, the bike lane may continue across with dotted lines, sometimes filled with green, as FHWA's green pavement approval describes. That crossing is where drivers and riders need to watch for each other.

The rule for riders in California

Vehicle Code 21208 says a person biking slower than the traffic around them must ride in the bike lane where one exists, but may leave it to pass, to prepare for a left turn, to avoid debris or hazards, or when approaching a place where a right turn is allowed. Riders may leave the lane only when it is reasonably safe, and must signal if a vehicle may be affected.

Parking protected bike lanes

Some protected bike lanes use parked cars as the barrier. FHWA's separated bike lane study lists parked cars among the vertical elements that can separate a bike lane, and the parked cars sit between the bike lane and moving traffic. The video above shows how drivers should use one.

A note on the law

This page explains the rules in plain words. It is not legal advice, and rules differ by state and city. For the exact rule where you live, read your state's vehicle code and any posted signs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drive in the bike lane to turn right?

In California, yes, within 200 feet of the intersection, under Vehicle Code 21209. Look for people on bikes before you merge in.

Can I drive in a bike lane to pass a car turning left?

Not under California Vehicle Code 21209. Passing is not one of the listed exceptions.

Do people on bikes have to ride in the bike lane?

In California, riders going slower than traffic must use the bike lane, with exceptions for passing, left turns, hazards, and approaching a right turn (Vehicle Code 21208).
