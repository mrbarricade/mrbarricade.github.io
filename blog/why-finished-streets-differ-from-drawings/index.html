Why a finished street differs from the drawing

By Vignesh Swaminathan, PE

Ever notice a new sidewalk or curb that is a little different from the drawing? That is normal.

Crews work with concrete, weather and real ground, so plans include a small allowed difference. Engineers pay extra attention where being off by a little matters, like ramps for wheelchairs and strollers.

Good plans are written for the people building them, so the finished street works as intended.
