Why moving a bus stop is not simple

By Vignesh Swaminathan, PE

Moving a bus stop sounds easy. Pick up the sign, put it down somewhere else.

But the new spot has to be safe to walk to, easy to board for people using wheelchairs, clear of driveways and poles, and good for the bus itself. The transit team and the street designers each see different problems, so the best results come when they plan together.

Next time a stop moves, there is probably a lot of teamwork behind it.
